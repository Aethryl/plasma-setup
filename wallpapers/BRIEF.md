# Wallpaper collection brief

Interview answers, kept for when the collection is built.

- **Imagery:** illustration, pixel art, sci-fi
- **Sci-fi:** retro-futurism (70s/80s paperback, Moebius, Syd Mead), space and planets, spaceships old, new and future
- **Busyness:** moderate, a clear subject but soft
- **Mood:** not tied to a mood, the desktop is rarely visible. Awe for the lock screen
- **Palette:** anything goes, no remapping required. The theme adapts to the wallpaper, not the reverse
- **Light/dark:** independent picks, no matched pairs
- **Format:** ultrawide 3440×1440 first; the 16:10 laptop may crop or use a different image
- **Sources:** free and curated packs (GitHub collections, Unsplash-like sites, KDE Store), Wallhaven, anything else worth pulling from
